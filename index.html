About

The systems work.

Because the person building them understands the mission, the money, the data, the message, and the people doing the work.

I build the systems that make fundraising and nonprofit operations work — the donor databases, grant-tracking frameworks, reporting infrastructure, automations, brand standards, and operating procedures that turn scattered activity into something visible and usable.

As the sole systems developer and operations administrator for a homeless-services nonprofit, I designed and ran the data environment behind more than $855,000 in annual revenue while managing donor operations, grant reporting, communications, privacy responsibilities, and cross-functional process design.

My work sits where technical architecture meets human judgment. I can map a Dataverse relationship, write a board-ready report, reconcile a payment workflow, shape a campaign, document the process, and explain why the system should exist in the first place.
